Örebro University · AASS / Örebro, Sweden

I separated registration algorithms, ROS interfaces and evaluation tools so experiments could be repeated and extended more easily. The work covered six-degree-of-freedom alignment of LiDAR and radar point clouds, with point-to-distribution and distribution-to-distribution matching.

Project / 01

3D perception & localization

Research internship · Örebro University

From legacy registration to a reusable ROS2 stack

The work began with a legacy NDT registration system. I separated the core matching algorithms, ROS interfaces and evaluation code into reusable parts, then extended registration to full six-degree-of-freedom LiDAR and radar alignment.

Point-to-distribution and distribution-to-distribution matching were evaluated with gradient, Newton and Ceres-based optimisation, including multi-resolution processing. PointCloud2 input, rosbag replay and RViz2 visualisation made experiments easier to inspect and reproduce.
